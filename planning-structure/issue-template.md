# Issue brief template

```markdown
---
key: BRK-XXX
title: Outcome-oriented imperative title
type: implementation
epic: EPIC-XX
sprint: SXX
status: Proposed
priority: P1
size: S
risk: Low
area: Area
depends_on: []
stable_key: breakout-cpp:issue=brk-xxx
---

## Objective

## Value / context

## In scope

## Out of scope

## Requirements

## Acceptance criteria

## Verification

## Dependencies

## Risks and decisions

## Stop conditions / escalation
```

Keep the brief about the behavior and boundary. Do not turn it into a
prescriptive design recipe or a list of methods to write.
