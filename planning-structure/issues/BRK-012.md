## Objective

Represent an active round and its terminal outcome explicitly, and transition to
game-over when the ball is lost or the brick field is cleared.

## Value / context

The C version uses ambiguous integer flags and blocks the application during the
round-end delay. A deliberate lifecycle boundary is needed before menus,
saving, and high-score recording can depend on game-over.

## In scope

- Typed or otherwise explicit round status.
- Ball-lost and all-bricks-cleared end conditions.
- A responsive timed transition, if the documented pause is retained.
- A stable game-over state that exposes final score and outcome to later UI.
- Reset/restart behavior for a fresh round.

## Out of scope

- Final text layout or music sting.
- Save-slot writes and high-score insertion.
- A complete main-menu route.

## Requirements

- The window continues to process events during any round-end pause.
- Both terminal conditions are observable and cannot silently leave the game
  loop running.
- A new round starts from a clean documented state rather than stale bricks or
  score.
- The win-versus-loss decision is recorded before this issue is marked Ready.

## Acceptance criteria

- Deliberately missing the paddle reaches game-over.
- Clearing all bricks reaches the chosen terminal outcome.
- The application remains responsive during the transition.
- A fresh start does not inherit the previous round's score or brick health.

## Verification

- Use a short-lived development setup or controlled play to trigger both end
  conditions.
- Test Escape/close during the terminal transition.
- Repeat a fresh round after game-over.

## Dependencies

BRK-011.

## Risks and decisions

Resolve the win-as-loss and round-end timing entries in the decision ledger. A
behavior change is acceptable only when explicitly chosen and documented.

## Stop conditions / escalation

Stop if game-over requires the text, audio, save, or score systems to be
implemented first. Expose a clean state boundary and leave presentation to the
later issues.
