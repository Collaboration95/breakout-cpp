## Objective

Record eligible completed games in the validated score table and provide the
documented high-score screen and navigation behavior.

## Value / context

This completes the score loop from a saved game to durable, visible feedback and
tests whether model-side effects remain separate from screen rendering.

## In scope

- Eligibility based on selected-slot/game context from the reference.
- One-time score recording at the appropriate game-over transition.
- High-score list presentation.
- Back navigation from high scores to the correct prior screen.
- Empty-table and invalid-entry display behavior.

## Out of scope

- Changing the score algorithm or file format.
- Audio changes.
- New ranking, statistics, or online leaderboard behavior.

## Requirements

- A score is not recorded repeatedly merely because game-over is redrawn.
- Quick games and saved games follow the chosen eligibility rule.
- The displayed table reflects validated sorted data.
- Back navigation honors the documented previous-screen behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

- A completed eligible game produces one visible top-three entry.
- An ineligible game follows the documented no-entry behavior.
- The high-score table remains correct after leaving and returning to it.
- The table survives relaunch through BRK-024's persistence path.

## Verification

- Complete eligible and ineligible rounds.
- Re-enter game-over/high-score screens and check for duplicates.
- Relaunch and inspect the retained ordering.

## Dependencies

BRK-022, BRK-024, and BRK-018.

## Risks and decisions

Keep score insertion out of render-only code. If eligibility differs between the
reference docs, record one rule before implementation.

## Stop conditions / escalation

Stop if the game-over state cannot identify whether it came from a saved slot or
quick game without adding hidden global state.
