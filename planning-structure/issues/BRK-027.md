## Objective

Connect screen and game events to the documented lobby, normal, intense, and
game-over audio behavior through one explicit audio state model.

## Value / context

The original audio behavior is stateful and includes a one-way intense-track
threshold. Making transitions explicit avoids scattered play/pause calls and
gives the learner a focused state-machine exercise.

## In scope

- Lobby music for menu states.
- Normal in-game track.
- Intense track at the documented score threshold.
- Game-over one-shot sound.
- Pause/resume or replacement behavior required by the reference flow.
- Transition behavior when returning between screens.

## Out of scope

- Volume settings.
- New tracks or dynamic music composition.
- Audio resource loading and ownership; BRK-026 owns that.

## Requirements

- A transition to a new audio context does not leave multiple unintended tracks
  playing.
- The intense-threshold behavior is deterministic and recorded if it differs
  from the original latch.
- The game-over sound is triggered by the transition, not by repeated redraws.
- Menu/game transitions retain the chosen pause/fade/replace semantics.

## Acceptance criteria

- Manual navigation demonstrates menu, normal-game, intense-game, and game-over
  audio behavior.
- Re-entering a screen does not restart or duplicate audio unexpectedly under
  the chosen policy.
- Score threshold behavior can be reproduced with a controlled test.

## Verification

- Exercise each screen transition.
- Cross the intense threshold and trigger game-over.
- Repeat game-over rendering and confirm the one-shot is not replayed merely by
  drawing the screen.

## Dependencies

BRK-026 and BRK-018.

## Risks and decisions

Resolve fade-versus-immediate and intense-latch choices in the ledger. Keep the
audio state boundary independent from UI labels.

## Stop conditions / escalation

Stop if transitions require audio calls embedded in every rendering function or
if missing audio resources make the intended policy impossible to test.
