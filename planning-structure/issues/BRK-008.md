## Objective

Make the moving ball interact with the left, right, and top field walls using
the documented geometry and bounce directions.

## Value / context

Wall collision is the first focused collision rule and provides an observable
check that the timing and coordinate choices are coherent.

## In scope

- The three non-terminal wall rectangles from the constants reference.
- Horizontal reflection at the side walls.
- Vertical reflection at the top wall.
- Any position correction needed to avoid immediate repeated collision.

## Out of scope

- Bottom-wall game-over behavior.
- Paddle or brick collision.
- Win/loss state management.

## Requirements

- The ball cannot pass through the intended side or top collision surfaces under
  normal movement.
- A side collision changes horizontal travel direction without changing the
  vertical component.
- A top collision changes vertical travel direction without changing the
  horizontal component.
- The documented wall geometry remains the source of truth.

## Acceptance criteria

- The ball repeatedly bounces from all three walls during a manual run.
- It does not become trapped oscillating against a wall after a collision.
- The behavior remains stable under the timing verification from BRK-007.

## Verification

- Exercise left, right, and top impacts separately.
- Test impacts at more than one angle.
- Record any collision-edge behavior that differs from the C reference.

## Dependencies

BRK-007.

## Risks and decisions

Keep bottom crossing observable for BRK-012 without treating it as a game-over
transition here. If collision correction changes the original feel, record that
choice rather than silently tuning it.

## Stop conditions / escalation

Stop if wall collision requires brick or paddle rules, or if the documented
rectangles appear inconsistent with the reference. Escalate the geometry issue.
