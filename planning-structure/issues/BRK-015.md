## Objective

Use the text and asset boundaries to make the active game and game-over state
communicate score, instructions, titles, and outcome on screen.

## Value / context

The core is technically playable after S3, but without feedback it is difficult
to resume, manually verify, or compare with the original. This issue gives the
learner a complete motivating vertical slice before menus and persistence.

## In scope

- The in-game score display.
- The start/instruction panel or equivalent documented prompt.
- Core screen titles and game-over score/outcome presentation.
- Documented font sizes and approximate positions.
- Separation between rendering the terminal screen and recording future scores.

## Out of scope

- Main-menu buttons and navigation.
- High-score persistence.
- Final text-cache optimization.

## Requirements

- Score is visible while a round is active.
- The player can tell when the round has not started, is active, or is over.
- Game-over rendering does not itself perform save or high-score side effects.
- The documented visual hierarchy and text content are used unless a decision
  records a deliberate change.

## Acceptance criteria

- A manual run shows the start prompt, live score, and game-over score.
- The text remains legible over the background at the documented locations.
- Re-entering or redrawing game-over does not duplicate model-side score effects.

## Verification

- Start a round, damage a brick, and inspect the score display.
- Trigger game-over and inspect final score/outcome.
- Reopen or redraw the terminal state and confirm no duplicate recording occurs.

## Dependencies

BRK-012 and BRK-014.

## Risks and decisions

Do not implement the high-score table here. This issue only exposes the current
round's information and creates the presentation seam later issues consume.

## Stop conditions / escalation

Stop if the screen cannot be rendered independently of persistence or if the
original text content is ambiguous in the reference docs.
